SSML quick reference

SSML must be wrapped in a single <speak> element. Tag support varies by engine — the generative and neural engines accept a narrower set than standard.

  • <break time="500ms"/> — pause
  • <emphasis level="strong">word</emphasis> — stress a word
  • <prosody rate="slow">text</prosody> — change speed or pitch
  • <say-as interpret-as="digits">2024</say-as> — read character by character
